# Watchtower Brand Style

## 1. Brand Positioning
Watchtower is a news intelligence workspace for high-stakes monitoring. The product promise is simple: detect signal early, prioritize urgency, and help teams respond faster.

Core message:
- Detect what matters first.
- Reduce noise with priority scoring.
- Act before stories escalate.

## 2. Color System
Use these as the primary palette (black/white allowed):

- `2f3e46` : deep brand base (primary dark backgrounds)
- `#656d4a` : primary action/brand accent
- `#ede0d4` : secondary surfaces/navigation depth
- `#a68a64` : warm emphasis (high-value CTA moments)
- `#7f5539` : utility text/contrast anchor

## 3. Typography
Current frontend intent:
- Display/headline: `Geist Sans` (`--font-display`)
- Body/UI text: `Inter` (`--font-body`)
- Mono/technical: `Monaspace (Sans or Xenon)`

Rules:
- Headlines use display font and tight tracking.
- Body copy stays concise and utility-driven.
- Avoid decorative type stacks not aligned to this system.

## 4. Visual Direction
Design feel: confident, editorial SaaS with operational clarity.

Principles:
- Strong contrast with dark-to-light section rhythm.
- Card-based information with subtle depth (`surface-panel` style).
- Accent color as punctuation, not background noise.
- Clear hierarchy over dense decoration.

## 5. Motion and Interaction
Keep motion functional and restrained:
- Use short transitions for hover/focus/state changes.
- Prefer meaningful animations that indicate hierarchy or state.
- playful and interactive mouse
- Keep essential flows snappy (auth, dashboard interactions).

Accessibility baseline:
- Preserve clear focus-visible states.
- Maintain readable contrast for text and controls.
- Do not communicate critical state by color alone.

## 6. Copywriting Voice
Voice attributes:
- Clear
- Creative
- Decisive
